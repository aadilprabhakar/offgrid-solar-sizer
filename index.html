Off-Grid Solar Sizer

Set your load for each hour of the day, pick a season and move the PV and battery sliders. The model runs a 24-hour day: PV feeds the load first and the surplus charges the battery. It checks that the battery carries you through the night and repeats day after day, with no grid.

Assumptions

Sun hours are kWh per kWp per day on the panel plane before system losses. Defaults are for Ludhiana, Punjab: PVGIS yield for a south-facing 25° array, averaged over summer (Apr–Jun), monsoon (Jul–Sep) and winter (Nov–Feb), then divided by the 78% system efficiency. Heavy cloud and fog days are not modelled.
